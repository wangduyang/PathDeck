This engine is an official pre-release. No Windhawk UI, service, tray or autorun is deployed.

TaskGlass.StartHost.exe is our small native x64 host. It uses the official GlobalHookSessionStart/HandleNewProcesses/End exports. It validates the local two-process filter and monitors TaskGlass/stop-event lifetime. There is no custom remote-memory or shellcode implementation. Sources/StartHost.cpp and build.ps1 are included.
